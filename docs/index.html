Policies

Privacy policy

TaskFlow stores everything you enter (tasks, money, profile and settings) only on your device. It does not collect, sell or share your data and has no ads or analytics. The app contacts the internet only to check for updates from the address you provide and, if you enable it, to read public contest schedules. Backups you create are files on your phone that you control. You can export or delete all your data at any time from this screen.

Terms of use

TaskFlow is provided as is, without warranty. Keep your own backups, because data on a lost or reset phone cannot be recovered by the developer. Reminders depend on your phone's notification and battery settings. By using the app you agree to use it lawfully and at your own risk.
